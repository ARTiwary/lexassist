RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord $1,450.00 per month, due on the first day of each month. A late fee of $75 applies to any payment received after the 5th.

2. Term. This lease begins on February 1, 2027 and continues for 12 months, ending January 31, 2028, unless renewed or terminated as provided herein.

3. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,450.00 prior to occupancy. Landlord shall return the deposit, less any lawful deductions, within 21 days after the end of the tenancy.

4. Termination. Landlord may terminate this lease at any time with 14 days written notice for any reason not prohibited by law. Tenant may terminate this lease only with 60 days written notice and payment of an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

5. Entry by Landlord. Landlord may enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection, maintenance, or to show the unit to prospective tenants.

6. Liability. Tenant assumes full liability for any and all damages to the premises, including damages caused by ordinary wear and tear, acts of nature, or third parties, regardless of fault.

7. Maintenance. Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance, including structural and system repairs (plumbing, electrical, HVAC), during the term of this lease.

8. Subletting. Tenant may not sublet or assign this lease without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold for any reason.

9. Governing Law. This lease shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the premises are located.
